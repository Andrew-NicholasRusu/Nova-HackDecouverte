# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive (Expo Router: iOS, Android, web)

## Users
Concordia University (Montréal) students on the Sir George Williams and Loyola campuses, checking their next class, finding rooms, seeing which friends are free, and reading campus conditions between classes.

## Product Purpose
Orbit is a campus dashboard: schedule, building/room navigation, friends' availability and student-reported campus status in one app.

## Brand commitments
Name stays "Orbit". Concordia burgundy #912338 (primary, on every screen) and gold #CBB576 (sparing highlights). Light and dark themes both supported.

## Open decisions
Inferred from the redesign brief, not interviewed. Dashboard schedule, friends and Campus Live data is sample data until Milestones 3, 5 and 6 land.
